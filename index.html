Campaign plan · Spec work

The Osetra Hour

A fall entertaining campaign built from one sentence, carried across Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, email and an in-store event.

The brief
We want to feature Osetra caviar for fall entertaining. Straight from the Great Atlantic job post. Here's what I'd bring back for review.
The big idea

“The Osetra Hour”

Fall hosting has a moment everyone loves: the half hour before dinner when guests arrive, coats come off and someone opens something good. We give that moment a name and put a tin of Osetra at the center of it.

It's a name people can say, a hashtag they can use and a ritual that repeats every weekend from October to New Year's. It also points straight at a product to sell: the Osetra Hour kit.

01

Make it approachable

Caviar on kettle chips, crispy potatoes and deviled eggs. Show that Osetra belongs at a Friday get-together, not only at a gala.

02

The host's playbook

Answer the questions hosts actually Google: how much per guest, what to serve it on, what to pour. Useful content gets saved and shared.

03

Gift the season

Hand off from hosting to gifting in November, so the campaign carries straight into holiday sales instead of stopping.

Instagram: the first nine

Tap a post to see the caption and why it's there. The grid is planned to read as one story at a glance.

Short-form video: three Reels & TikToks

Ranking the least fancy things to put caviar on

“Caviar on…, ranked”

A trend format that sells with humor. High-low pairings make Osetra feel fun and get comments arguing about the ranking.

  • Hook text over a chip held up to camera
  • Five fast tastes: kettle chip, tater tot, deviled egg, smashed potato, blini
  • Final pick revealed with a spoon to camera
  • End card: “Which one are you serving?”
POV: it's 6pm and your guests are early

Setting the Osetra Hour

Quiet ASMR: ice crunching, the tin opening, a cork popping. No voiceover, so it plays on mute and loops cleanly.

  • Ice poured into a bowl, close and loud
  • Tin lid lifted, slow push in
  • Mother-of-pearl spoon, one scoop
  • Doorbell. Cut to black with the title.
How much caviar do I need for 8 people?

Host math

Talking-head plus overhead B-roll. Answers a real question and drives straight to the right tin size.

  • Question on screen, said to camera
  • The rule of thumb, with the tins lined up on the counter
  • Three ways to stretch a tin across a party
  • “Save this for your next dinner party”
