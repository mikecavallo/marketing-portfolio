# Image prompts for the portfolio

There are 10 images. Save each one with the exact filename shown, as a JPG under 400 KB (squoosh.app shrinks them in seconds). The site picks them up on its own. Until a file exists, its slot shows a placeholder with the filename on it.

All 10 go in `img/gen/`. They're the Osetra Hour campaign photos.

## How to use these

Each prompt is written to paste straight in. It works in Midjourney, ChatGPT (image), Gemini, Ideogram or Flux.

- **Midjourney:** paste the prompt, then add the parameters line under it.
- **ChatGPT, Gemini or Ideogram:** paste the prompt and say the aspect ratio in words ("make it portrait 4:5").
- Make 4 versions of each and keep the best. Pick for one consistent mood across the set rather than the single flashiest frame.

### The look of the whole shoot (already built into every prompt)

Dark, warm, candlelit fall. The palette is charcoal, deep forest green, amber candlelight and brass, with cream linen as the only light surface. Shoot like a food magazine: real textures, natural shadows, shallow depth of field, a little film grain. No props that shout "stock photo".

### Getting the caviar right

Bad AI caviar is the most common failure, and a caviar company will spot it instantly. Real osetra pearls are medium-sized, round, separate and glossy, and their color runs dark grey to olive-brown with a golden sheen. They're never jet-black jelly, never shiny wet paste and never teal. If a result looks like black tapioca or fish-egg goo, throw it out.

### Throw these out every time

- extra or melted fingers
- text, logos or brand names on any tin
- caviar served in a metal spoon (it's always mother-of-pearl, bone or horn)
- plastic-looking food
- heavy HDR glow
- plates floating with no shadow
